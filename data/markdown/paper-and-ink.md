# Paper and Ink

**Author:** 

**Published:** 2026-09-14

**Categories:** 

**Tags:** 

**Source:** https://blog.billymacdeus.com/

---

Ink overflowing on a pen, bleeding
Blue taints on white paper, spreading
Uncontrollably on its surface, soaking
Inevitably as if a rape, is happening
from the ink uniting in to the whiteness of the paper
My hand smudged
I rubbed it to erase the ink
All the more it expanded
Extending, further, blotching
My skin
-Mac
