Visits
frequently
Needs some
distraction
To get my
footing
Onward
Despite
The bearable
woe
As if it
consumes
Especially
These lone
times
Of thinking
bleak
Almost
Losing hope
To where
shall I turn
Fostering
Strength
Once again
Fighting the
ebb
Shunning the
other side
