# Happy Birthday Mother 2013

**Author:** 

**Published:** 2026-09-14

**Categories:** 

**Tags:** 

**Source:** https://blog.billymacdeus.com/

---

To my beloved mother, another year has passed. Gone are the days and months, it's another chapter to unfold in your journey.
It makes me sad though that you're celebrating today, Monday, inside the hospital.
You were strong in thoughts, and I can see it in your eyes, fighting further, even if you aren't speaking the pain you're having. It breaks my heart to note that you're undergoing all these, turmoils and sicknesses. I was dreaming that you should be enjoying your old age. Pure bliss and happiness, but, you were not spared.
Yet, I believe ur courage, your persistence, your endurance - this has long been a yoke. But you never complained, you accepted it all, you were brave in fighting.
I love you mother, happy birthday!
Keep fighting, keep living!
MAC
