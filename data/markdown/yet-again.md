# Yet Again

**Author:** 

**Published:** 2026-09-14

**Categories:** 

**Tags:** 

**Source:** https://blog.billymacdeus.com/

We are growing old
Yet again the night wind was so cold
It left me feeling blue
All the more because I needed you
To draw my destiny
With full mutiny
And distinctive veiny
Stars I cannot see
