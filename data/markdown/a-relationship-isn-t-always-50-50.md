# A Relationship Isn't Always 50/50

**Author:** 

**Published:** 2026-09-14

**Categories:** 

**Tags:** 

**Source:** https://blog.billymacdeus.com/

---

The Love Clinic said it nicely. Sa isang relasyon, hindi sa lahat ng pagkakataon, smooth sailing. There are fights, sometimes, the boat is sinking, and there are rough edges to smoothen out.
Ngunit ang napakaganda above it all ay kung ang pag-ibig ang nagbunsod para i-overcome ang mga dumarating at darating pang mga "tests", it will all be matter of a tandemized diskarte between two hearts that care for each other.
I know hindi siya natatapos, ganun naman talaga eh, level-up ng level up, in the end, if you both end up victorious because of the collective efforts and fighting a good fight displayed by both sides, mas lalong tatatag ang inyong pundasyon - a meaningful one.
~billymacdeus
