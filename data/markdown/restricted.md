And you kept coming
back

persistent

So I rested my case
I thought it was
over
But you said, it has
just started

But you just laugh
I tried to explain
And then you
shrugged
I tried to lock my
gaze
Yet you vowed your
head
I tried to kiss you
Then you can't be
moved
I tried to say
goodbye
You din't believe me
I tried to negotiate
But you gave no
options
I asked forgiveness
You just smiled
I told you I'm dead
serious
You:poker faced,
then laughed
I asked you out
You said, yes or no?
I told you it was
half-meant

hand

gone.
