# The 3 Basics of Stoicism: Perceptions, Actions, Will

The big three : Perception, Action and Will are the basics - foundations of executing the Stoic life. They are overlapping to each other  but each has their critical power and discpline.
It's like a three-legged stool, don't let one stool go down, otherwise it will tumble down.
“ the following little reminder sums up the three most essential parts of Stoic philosophy worth carrying with you every day, into every decision:
Control your perceptions.
Hindi mo kontrolado ang mga nangyayari sa paligid, pero kaya mong kontrolin kung paano mo ito titingnan at tatanggapin. Ang tamang mindset ang susi sa tamang reaksyon.
Pangalagaan ang iyong pananaw.
Direct your actions properly.
Ituon ang iyong kilos sa tama.
Ang kontrolado mo lang ay ang iyong mga desisyon at aksyon. Gawin ang nararapat at huwag sayangin ang enerhiya sa walang saysay.
Willingly accept what’s outside your control.
Matutong tanggapin ang hindi mo kontrolado.
Hindi lahat ng bagay ay kayang baguhin o pigilan. Mag-focus sa kaya mong gawin at tanggapin nang maluwag ang hindi mo maimpluwensyahan.
Excerpt From: Holiday, Ryan. “The Daily Stoic.” Apple Books.
