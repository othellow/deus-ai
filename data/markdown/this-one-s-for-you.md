# This One's for You!

To breathe the air of careless thoughts
Freedom denounces further laws
Verbose-like smiles to draw the tamed fierceness
Forever marked in the recesses of memory
Advances to decrypt the mystery
Nowhere but hear the symphony
Of the music endlessly repeating
Like the waves that forever sprawling
Missing like no other part can replace
'twas like the shying of the moon
Behind the clouds as it drapes to blackness
Into the silence of a windless night
Beyond...
Beyond the everlast
MAC
(For the mind that can decipher the intricacies of life)
