Ingenious Robin Sloan - the author of this dazzling book makes me want to meet him and discuss this great work of fiction.
I'm baffled and at the same time entwined irresistibly to turn page after page of the book. Its wit and techno-geeky spasms mixed with ancient and historical inventions were conveyed in a delicious waft of pure enjoyment.
This is wayyy better than the Da Vinci Code of Dan Brown.
-MAC
