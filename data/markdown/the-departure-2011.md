# The Departure 2011

**Published:** 2026-09-14

**Categories:** 

**Tags:**

It has always been a fact that saying goodbye is at
it's
its hardest when it seems that a piece of you is going to be left behind.
Saying farewell is one of the most agonizing feeling, that spur of the moment when you have to depart, one must go, the other must let go.
I have observed that even the most stoney-hearted person could succumb to a meltdown. And when that time comes,
everything's gonna be alright kind-of-feeling
slowly envelopes into a no-drama picturesque.
When a door is closed, a window shall be opened.
-MAC
