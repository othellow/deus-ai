# Mind and the Battle of Imagination

**Author:** 

**Published:** 2026-09-14

**Categories:** 

**Tags:** 

**Source:** https://blog.billymacdeus.com/

---

Oh lips...
So tender
Allow me to gently
kiss.. That sparkling gloss
to let me feel
the warmth of
your breath that
I wish to hear
To let my heart
Murmur
in your ear...
Music of
solemn praise
Reaching to your
Heart
Vibrating to your mind
Until such time
That the waves
Of intensity
Shall be
In unison to mine
Where I can
Finally touch you
And embrace you
While we lose
Ourselves
In to
the oblivion
Of eternal passion...
-mac
(Just about to sleep, thinking of wonderland and the temptations that alight in the blurry vision of this vulnerable soul, yet with the strongest heart to fail not HIS expectations)
Don't forget to pray before you snuggle in slumber...
