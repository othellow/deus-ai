For those who belong to the BB Team, here's a hidden app on your smartphone that you'll find useful esp when you're at  wits end in sorting out if the problem is a hardware or software.
There's actually a self test feat of this sleek and beautiful phone, whatever model you've got, as long as you're running a software version of 4.something or later, then you can run this hidden app from your phone.
Here's how:
1. Click on
OPTIONS
icon from the homescreen
2. Scroll down all the way to
STATUS
, then click it
3. Once you're on the
status screen,
just type TEST from your keypad and it should bring up the menu for the self-test application.
4. Select
Start,
and you're good to go, you must see something like this:
It's kinda helpful and could provide insights to better judgment from current issues you're experiencing at hand.
One of the downside though that pisses me off, the test is INTERACTIVE, all i thought before was, it could run on its own, however, you must need to attend to it and answer with a yes or no for the tests that you selected to run, this of course is based on your perception whether the output was satisfied as desired by the instructions given.
