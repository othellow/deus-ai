Here are some of the most widely recognized styles of love:
Eros: This type of love is often associated with passion and physical attraction. It is characterized by intense emotions and a desire for physical intimacy. Eros is commonly seen in the early stages of romantic relationships.
An example of eros love might be the intense attraction and passion that two people feel for each other when they first start dating. They might feel a strong physical and emotional connection and be driven by the desire to be close to each other.
Ludus: Ludus is a playful and flirtatious type of love that is more focused on enjoyment and pleasure than commitment or emotional depth. It can involve casual dating, one-night stands, and other non-serious relationships.
Ludus love might involve a casual dating relationship, where two people are more focused on having fun and enjoying each other's company than building a serious emotional connection. This type of love might not involve a lot of commitment or future planning.
Storge: This type of love is often referred to as companionate love and is based on deep emotional connections and mutual respect between partners. Storge is commonly seen in long-term relationships and marriages.
An example of storge love might be a couple who have been together for many years and have built a deep emotional connection based on mutual respect and shared experiences. They might value each other's companionship and support, and their love might be less focused on passion and more focused on comfort and stability.
Pragma: Pragma is a pragmatic and practical type of love that is based on shared values and common goals. It often involves careful consideration and planning and is common in arranged marriages.
A couple who have similar career goals and values might exemplify pragma love. They might have chosen each other based on shared interests and compatibility, rather than just physical attraction or intense emotions.
Mania: Mania is an obsessive and possessive type of love that is characterized by intense emotions and a need for control. It can often lead to jealousy and possessiveness in relationships.
Mania love might be seen in a couple where one partner is possessive and jealous, and constantly seeks to control the other partner's actions and behaviors. This type of love can be unhealthy and destructive, leading to arguments and misunderstandings.
Agape: Agape is a selfless and unconditional type of love that is focused on giving rather than receiving. It is commonly associated with religious or spiritual beliefs and is often seen in acts of charity and compassion.
An example of agape love might be someone who volunteers regularly at a homeless shelter or donates money to charity. They might be motivated by a deep sense of compassion and selflessness, wanting to help others without expecting anything in return.
Philautia: Philautia is a self-love or self-care type of love that involves taking care of oneself and valuing one's own well-being. It is an essential type of love that forms the foundation for all other types of love.
Taking care of oneself physically and mentally is an important aspect of philautia love. This might involve eating healthy foods, getting enough sleep, practicing self-care, and cultivating positive relationships with others. By valuing and prioritizing one's own well-being, individuals can better show up for others and form more fulfilling relationships.
Research has identified several distinct styles of love, each with its own unique characteristics and qualities. Understanding these different styles can help us recognize our own preferences and tendencies in relationships and develop more fulfilling and satisfying partnerships.
Here are some details on how love changes over time:
Love changes over time because humans are dynamic and constantly evolving beings. As we grow and change, so do our needs, desires, and expectations in relationships.
The initial stage of romantic love, known as the "honeymoon phase," is characterized by intense passion, excitement, and infatuation. During this stage, we are often blinded by love and may overlook our partner's flaws or incompatibilities.
As time goes on, the honeymoon phase fades, and couples transition into a more stable, long-term phase of love. In this stage, the focus shifts from passion and infatuation to building a strong, healthy partnership based on trust, respect, and commitment.
During this stage, couples may experience challenges, conflicts, and hardships. However, how they respond to these challenges can determine the strength and longevity of their relationship.
Love can also change over time due to external factors such as life events, stress, and societal expectations. For example, becoming a parent can change the dynamic of a romantic relationship and require couples to adapt to new roles and responsibilities.
Love can also change over time due to individual growth and development. As individuals pursue personal goals and interests, their priorities and values may shift, and they may require different things from their relationship.
However, with open communication, mutual respect, and a willingness to grow and evolve together, love can continue to flourish over time.
Love is a dynamic and ever-changing experience that requires effort, commitment, and adaptability. As we navigate the ups and downs of life and relationships, it's important to remember that love can continue to grow and evolve as long as we are willing to grow and evolve with it.
