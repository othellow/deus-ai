Kahapon, magka chat kami ng isa kong kaibigan, she was asking me what's the difference between #attitude and #character.
At first, i was taken aback kase nag-isip talaga ako ng malalim paanu yun sasagutin. One absolute, immediate answer came to my mind -- character is a deeper portrayal of virtues and vices (if you may) whereas, attitude is the way a person deals with things and situations at hand.
That then inspired me to write this post. Napapanahon at sana'y makatulong ito sa ating lahat.
