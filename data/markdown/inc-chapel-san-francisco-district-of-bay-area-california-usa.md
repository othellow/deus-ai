W
e were browsing social media yesterday and came across with the feed of
INC Edifices of Faith
which we are also following from our
QTPodcast
FB Page.
It felt compelling to share and make a write-up, about these majestic and pleasing-to-the-eyes chapels of the Church of Christ (Iglesia Ni Cristo) -- such an awe to behold, these wonderful structures, these houses of worship, these beautiful architecture... which are the sacred locations to bring praises and glory, to the one true Almighty!
O
ur eyes immediately caught the magnificent and magnetic aura of this INC Chapel in the Locale Congregation of San Francisco, District of Bay Area California USA. The Parthenon-like structure at a first glance came rushing to our minds because of its fronting colossal columns. We were amazed by its appeal and charm.
Nakaka-proud talaga to gaze at this monumental edifice, kahit sa images lang, there's this powerful impression to really take a closer look and scrutinize... it's like you get the sudden feeling na sana andun ka to witness it real-time in your own eyes -- the feels and the thrills, of beholding such a momentous experience.
S
ince we cannot get enough of our amazement and wonder, we tried to google and research more about the location and its street view. True enough, the building is really enourmous - whether top view or angle-wise, the appeal is always there, at hindi siya nakakasawang tingnan. It's as if, you wanted to experience going there inside, kaagad-agad.
W
e hope to hear more facts and trivia about this Local Congregation from the District of Bay Area California USA, but that is all for now. There is this longing to somehow, someday in the future, we get to experience and touchdown on this temple, this house of prayer... to further feel its calming impact to our hearts, that someday soon, we hope to get inside; kneel and pray, give praises and sing, but most of all, to worship the God Almighty - and render thanksgiving.
