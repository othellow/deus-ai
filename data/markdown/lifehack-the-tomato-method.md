Strength
is how hard you can throw a tomato,
Dexterity
is the ability to cut a tomato without cutting yourself,
Constitution
is being able to eat a rotten tomato,
Intelligence
is knowing that a tomato is a fruit,
While
wisdom
is knowing not to put in a fruit salad,
Charisma
is the ability to sell a tomato-based fruit salad.
And as a bonus,
luck
is your ability to find a tomato in a field of potatoes.
