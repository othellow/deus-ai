# Sleepless in June's Heat

**Author:** 

**Published:** 2026-09-14

**Categories:** 

**Tags:** 

**Source:** https://blog.billymacdeus.com/

---

Elusive, O
you slumber!
Mind at play
Body in
disarray
Sweat drippin
From the heat
smokin'
With the
hammering thuds
Of the nearby
fixture
All the more
The eyes to
stare
On  the white ceiling
Come now
O sleep
To peace I
shall rest
But nowhere
Are you
passing
None at all
My stupor
Acquainted to
jam
My creative
thinking
MAC
