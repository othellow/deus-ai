# Letters of Juliet from Romeo

**Author:** 

**Published:** 2026-09-14

**Categories:** 

**Tags:** 

**Source:** https://blog.billymacdeus.com/

---

You are tempting ...
leaving me with gasps, and then i shall wait
for you to utter again, the words i long to hear
'ven by a ping of text, i crumble in delight
of the joy you bring, your compliments
the eagerness, for you to meet me
in a mask, in a veil of eager glory
just one time, you beg
for what if, my defenses are low
shall we dance in to the tune
of everlasting gore
or keep the promises, neither we have spoken
and only the wind can tell
by your chuckle, when's the last time?
i couldn't remember
the last time we met, again in person
back in 2008? in the pelted streets
of Vergara, or in the bridge
and yes! in the gates of heaven
don't take it, i'm dead
serious!
if you want to, don't chase it
be the beacon, of light from darkness
