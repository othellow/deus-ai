your pure white teeth

asking, questioning, waiting

incidentally
in a non essential way
to check
the thermometer

to my body's dictation
you were amazed
you set the trap
you walked..
slowly, lookin me back
with a wink
seducing
your eyes, so wonderful
i followed you

with mischief

with your hot lips
