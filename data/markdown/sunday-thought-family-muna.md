# Sunday Thought: Family Muna

**Published:** 2026-09-14

**Categories:** 

**Tags:** 

**Source:** https://blog.billymacdeus.com/

---

Pay close attention to those you care about.
Sometimes,
when a loved one says,
"I'm OK"
they need you to look them in the eyes,
hug them tight,
and reply:
"I know you're not"
And don't be too upset if some people only seem to
remember you
when they need you.
Feel privileged that you are like a beacon of light that
comes to their minds when there is darkness in their lives.
