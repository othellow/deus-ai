# Positive Outlook

---

Positive outlook, is a choice.
It was a learned lesson today, in our Sunday worship service.
It's given that when everything goes smoothly in our lives, happiness reigns. But, is that always the case?
Are you brave enough to accept the reality that it's not only the good things we receive in life that make the journey worth it, but also the trials that are thrown on us?
Besides, we should not only accept the positive things happening in our lives, but most of all, to embrace the thought of having stricken with grief and pain.
To achieve hope, there's a simple progressive formula. Sufferings make faith and hope perfect, how?
Suffering creates patience, patience creates perseverance. Perseverance creates character, character creates hope.
The constant endurance of suffering will make our faith complete. And who are we to say no to sufferings, let's put to contrast that blessings are not measured only in good tidings but of the equal opposite.
